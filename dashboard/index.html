How the value is built

Implied value = TVL × sector multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier. The sector multiple follows the protocol's role: 0.50 for exchange-like protocols (DEXs, lending, CDPs, derivatives, perpetuals) whose token would capture ongoing fees; 0.40 for allocators (vaults, curators, yield aggregators); 0.30 for asset and utility protocols (RWA, wrappers, payments, chains) and for any category the model does not name. The growth multiplier is 1 + 0.6 × the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%; a protocol with no seven-day change scores 1. The maturity multiplier runs from 0.70 for a protocol listed yesterday to 1.00 for one listed four years ago, so age helps but never dominates; a protocol with no listing date scores as if it were a year old.

implied = tvl_usd × sector(category) × (1 + 0.6 × clamp(change_7d, −60, 120) / 100) × (0.70 + 0.30 × min(age_days, 1460) / 1460)

The farm plan for a row is generated from the same numbers: the capital follows the protocol's TVL, the gas follows its main chain, and the hold period follows its age. The plan tells you what to do, what it costs and how long it takes — it does not tell you a token is coming. Nothing here is financial advice, and no row is a promise of an airdrop.
